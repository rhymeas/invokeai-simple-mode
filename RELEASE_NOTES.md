# InvokeAI Simple Mode v1.3.1

This patch adds direct mouse-wheel and trackpad zoom to the image in Focus Edit. Move the pointer over the image and scroll upward to zoom in or downward to zoom out. The page and image stage remain fixed while zooming.

Wheel zoom uses the same 20% to 400% range as the plus and minus buttons. Trackpad movement is scaled continuously for finer control, while a conventional mouse wheel produces clear steps.
